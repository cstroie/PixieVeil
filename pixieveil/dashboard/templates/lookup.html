Patient lookup

Find every study received for a patient and the IDs it has on RHYTHM, e.g. to answer a data-erasure request. Matches exactly on original or pseudonymized PatientID / StudyInstanceUID, study number, RHYTHM Repository Study ID, job id or batch. Works after a study's files are purged.
