Scanners & protocols

Each scanner has its own protocols. A study is matched to its scanner by manufacturer and model, and to a protocol by the exact protocol name the scanner sends (or the protocol part of the study description, e.g. Head^CAP_SPIRAL_CONTRAST (Adult) → CAP_SPIRAL_CONTRAST). A match fills in the study's RHYTHM protocol and indication automatically.
